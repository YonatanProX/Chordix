Our commitment

We regard accessibility of the Service to all users, including people with disabilities, as a core value. The site was built with the aim of meeting the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 5773-2013, Israeli Standard SI 5568, and the WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines.

What has been made accessible

  • Full keyboard navigation, including a “skip to main content” link and clear focus indication (which can be further emphasised in the accessibility menu).
  • A persistent accessibility menu: larger text, light mode, high contrast, dyslexia-friendly reading spacing, colour-blind friendly mode and reduced animation.
  • Respect for the system preference for reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion).
  • Semantic structure, ARIA labels for buttons and dialogs, and screen-reader announcements of state changes such as analysis completion, errors or a change of the playing chord.
  • Information is never conveyed by colour alone: alongside colour, the section name, chord name and time are displayed, and in colour-blind mode a visual pattern is added.
  • A display adapted to small screens and to browser zoom.

Known limitations

The timeline and structure-map components are graphical components based on position in time, and their operation with a screen reader is limited; alongside them, a textual “chord sheet” view and a “copy sheet as text” option are always available, providing the same information in an accessible form. The audio player is a built-in browser component and is subject to its accessibility behaviour. We are continuing to improve.
